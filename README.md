# Customer-Shopping-Behavior-Analysis


## Project Overview

Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis is a data analytics project aimed at understanding customer purchasing patterns, spending habits, product preferences, and seasonal sales trends. The project utilizes Python for data cleaning and exploratory data analysis, SQL for querying and extracting business insights, and Power BI for creating an interactive dashboard.

The objective is to transform raw shopping transaction data into meaningful insights that can help retail businesses improve customer engagement, optimize sales strategies, and make data-driven decisions.

---

## Problem Statement

Retail businesses collect large volumes of customer transaction data every day. However, identifying purchasing trends, customer preferences, and factors influencing sales can be challenging. Without proper analysis, valuable business opportunities may remain hidden.

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior data to uncover patterns related to customer demographics, product categories, seasonal purchasing trends, subscription status, payment preferences, and revenue generation. The insights obtained can support better marketing strategies and business growth.

---

## Objectives

- Perform data cleaning and preprocessing using Python.
- Conduct exploratory data analysis (EDA) to identify trends and patterns.
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior using SQL queries.
- Create interactive Power BI dashboards for business reporting.
- Generate actionable insights for decision-making.

## Key Insights

- Product categories contribute differently to overall revenue generation.
- Seasonal trends significantly influence customer purchasing behavior.
- Customer subscription status impacts purchasing frequency and spending patterns.
- Payment methods indicate customer transaction preferences.
- Certain locations generate higher revenue compared to others.
- Discounts and promotional offers influence customer purchasing decisions.
